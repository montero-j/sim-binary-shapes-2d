Figure SM-avalanche-ccdf shows the empirical complementary cumulative distribution $P(S \geq s)$ of avalanche sizes for the four mixtures at $R_a = 0.1$, for the pure systems ($\chi = 0$) and for $\chi = 0.2$. Fitting the tail ($s > 0.5\langle s \rangle$) to $A\,e^{-s/s^*}$ gives $s^*/\langle s \rangle$ between 0.89 and 1.29 (Table SX). The tails are exponential in all cases, and 6 of the 8 distributions collapse onto $e^{-x}$ when $s$ is rescaled by $\langle s \rangle$ ($|s^*/\langle s \rangle - 1| \leq 0.15$). The deviations come from an excess of very small avalanches with respect to the exponential tail extrapolated down to $s = 1$: Disk-Disk at $\chi = 0$ (27\%), Square-Disk at $\chi = 0$ (24\%), Triangle-Disk at $\chi = 0.2$ (12\%), Triangle-Disk at $\chi = 0$ (7\%), Disk-Disk at $\chi = 0.2$ (7\%). These extra small avalanches lower $\langle s \rangle$ and raise $s^*/\langle s \rangle$ above one, without changing the exponential form of the tail.
